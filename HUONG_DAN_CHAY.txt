HUONG DAN CHAY DU AN FAST&FAT
=============================

1. CAU TRUC THU MUC
-------------------

  simulator/   Gia lap CAC HE THONG CUA DOANH NGHIEP (may POS, app, san Grab/Shopee, so kho,
               may cham cong). Chi dung de tao ra file du lieu tho, thay cho du lieu that
               khong thu thap duoc. KHONG thuoc pipeline DE.
  data/        Du lieu. Moi tap co:
                 source_systems/   file tho do cac he thong xuat ra (JSON, CSV, Excel)
                 warehouse/        kho DuckDB: schema bronze, silver, gold
                 ground_truth/     dap an cac su co (chi dung de danh gia, pipeline KHONG doc)
               Hai tap: calibration = tap phat trien, holdout = tap kiem tra.
  pipeline/    DE - nap file tho vao tang Bronze (ingest.py), chay dbt (run_dbt.py).
  dbt/         DE + DA - bien doi bang SQL: Bronze -> Silver (lam sach) -> Gold (kho hinh sao,
               bang chi so), kem kiem thu du lieu.
  analytics/   DA - bo chi so giam sat va bo phat hien (doc tang Gold).
  app/         Ung dung Streamlit duy nhat: phan tich van hanh, quan ly du lieu, danh gia.
  tools/       Tien ich: mo kho DuckDB bang trinh SQL (open_warehouse.ps1).
  docs/        Bao cao, tai lieu.

  Luong du lieu:

  [simulator] -> source_systems/ (file tho) -> Bronze -> Silver -> Gold -> truy vet, bao cao
   gia lap         dau vao cua pipeline       |---------- pipeline DE + DA ----------|
   doanh nghiep

2. CAI DAT (1 lan)
------------------

  Python 3.12, tao moi truong ao trong thu muc du an:
    python -m venv .venv
    .venv\Scripts\pip install -r requirements.txt

3. CHAY TU DAU (da chay san, chi lam lai khi can)
-------------------------------------------------

  B1. Tao du lieu nguon 182 ngay (~3,5 phut moi tap):
    .venv\Scripts\python -m simulator.run --profile calibration --days 182 --reset
    .venv\Scripts\python -m simulator.run --profile holdout --days 182 --reset

  B2. Nap file tho vao Bronze (~80 giay):
    .venv\Scripts\python -m pipeline.ingest --profile calibration

  B3. Silver + Gold + kiem thu (~1-2 phut, ket qua mong doi: PASS=211 ERROR=0):
    .venv\Scripts\python -m pipeline.run_dbt --profile calibration build

  (Lam B2, B3 voi --profile holdout cho tap kiem tra.)

4. THEM 1 NGAY DU LIEU MOI (mo phong van hanh hang ngay)
---------------------------------------------------------

    .venv\Scripts\python -m simulator.run --profile calibration --days 1
    .venv\Scripts\python -m pipeline.ingest --profile calibration
    .venv\Scripts\python -m pipeline.run_dbt --profile calibration build

  Bronze chi nap cac file moi (nhan biet bang ma bam), chay lai khong bi trung.

5. MO UNG DUNG (mot dia chi duy nhat cho ca phan du lieu va phan phan tich)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Cach 1 - Docker, bat ca he thong bang mot lenh:
    docker compose up -d --build
    http://localhost:8501   ung dung
    http://localhost:8080   Airflow (admin / admin) - cung xem/chay duoc ngay trong ung dung
    http://localhost:8600   API gia lap cua phan mem POS dam may va App (tai lieu API: /docs)

  Cach 2 - chay ung dung tren may:
    .venv\Scripts\streamlit run app\main.py --server.port 8504

  Cac nhom trang (xep theo dong du lieu; trang chu co "Hanh trinh cua du lieu" 13 buoc de trinh dien):
    Nguon du lieu      : Ket noi nguon (3 kenh, trang thai tung nguon, bo ket noi API, tep can xu ly),
                         Tai tep len (gui tep, hop dong du lieu, tu choi kem ly do), Tep nguon (xem tep tho)
    Du lieu            : Van hanh hang ngay, Kho du lieu (Bronze/Silver/Gold + SQL),
                         Mo hinh va pha he (lineage, luoc do hinh sao tu dbt), Chat luong du lieu
    Phan tich van hanh : Tong quan (Metabase), Trung tam canh bao, Truy vet nguyen nhan (co Bang chung goc ->
                         mo dung tep, dung dong o trang Tep nguon), Tro ly phan tich
    Kiem chung         : Bo mo phong (mot ngay, bay goc nhin; kich ban su co - DOC DAP AN),
                         Danh gia bo phat hien (ket qua 2 tap, do nhay theo nguong va theo cach tinh muc ky vong)
  Chon moi truong o thanh ben: Van hanh (mac dinh) / Danh gia - tap phat trien / Danh gia - tap kiem tra.

  Mo kho bang trinh SQL (chi doc):  powershell -File tools\open_warehouse.ps1

6. KIEM CHUNG BO MO PHONG (doc dap an - chi de kiem tra du lieu nguon da tao dung)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    .venv\Scripts\python -m simulator.validate --profile calibration

7. CHAY TU DONG HANG NGAY (moi truong van hanh thu "live")
---------------------------------------------------------

  Tap live = ban sao tap phat trien, chi dung cho van hanh hang ngay; KHONG dung de danh gia
  (tap phat trien va tap kiem tra giu nguyen de ket qua danh gia khong doi).

  Tao tap live (1 lan):
    .venv\Scripts\python -m pipeline.run_daily --init-live

  Hai vai tro tach rieng, giong mot doanh nghiep that:
    - He thong nguon (bo mo phong):
        POS dam may (8 chi nhanh) va App ghi vao kho cua phan mem data/live/_he_thong_nguon/, ben ngoai chi lay
        duoc qua API (source_api/, cong 8600: ma Bearer, phan trang, gioi han tan suat, thinh thoang loi 503);
        POS cai tai may (CN5, CN7, CN10), cham cong, kho, san, thoi tiet xuat tep vao data/live/source_systems/
        .venv\Scripts\python -m pipeline.run_daily --profile live --step simulate --days 7
    - Pipeline: extract -> check -> ingest -> quality -> dbt -> detect -> publish
        extract = bo ket noi (pipeline/extract.py) keo tang dan qua API theo moc ngay, trang thai o
        data/live/landing/api_connector.json; API khong phan hoi thi cac nguon tep van chay tiep
        .venv\Scripts\python -m pipeline.run_daily --profile live        (khong co du lieu moi thi dung ngay)
        .venv\Scripts\python -m pipeline.extract --profile live --since 2024-09-23   (lay lai qua API tu mot ngay)
    - Chay API tren may khong qua Docker: .venv\Scripts\uvicorn source_api.app:app --port 8600
  Tep do nguoi gui (bao cao san, so kho, kiem ke, ban sua): trang "Tai tep len", hoac tep mau o samples\tep_mau\
  Thu nghiem "ngay xau" (11 tinh huong tep xau + API sap roi song lai, khoang 6 phut, tu khoi phuc live):
    .venv\Scripts\python tools\thu_tinh_huong.py   -> docs\results\TINH_HUONG_BAT_THUONG.md

  Chay bang Airflow trong Docker (can mo Docker Desktop truoc):
    docker compose up -d --build        (lan dau build image khoang 3 phut)
    http://localhost:8080               tai khoan admin / admin
      DAG "fastfat_nguon"    : he thong nguon, 23:00 moi ngay (hoac bam tu ung dung)
      DAG "fastfat_pipeline" : TU CHAY khi DAG nguon xuat tep (Airflow Dataset), va kiem tra them moi gio
    docker compose down                 (tat)
  Moi luot xu ly ghi nhat ky vao ops.pipeline_runs, canh bao vao ops.alerts.
  Xem va dieu khien tren app: trang "Van hanh hang ngay".

  Demo "du lieu moi -> he thong tu xu ly": tap live co cai san 2 su co minh hoa tu 23/09/2024
  (simulator/scenarios/live.json: L1 thieu nhan su ca toi CN3, L2 ShopeeFood thu hoa hong cao CN1).
    .venv\Scripts\python tools\chuan_bi_demo.py --khoi-phuc    (dua tap live ve 22/09/2024, khoang 20-70 giay)
    Trang "Van hanh hang ngay" -> chon 7 ngay -> bam "Gui du lieu ngay moi" -> khoang 3,5 phut
    -> hien 2 canh bao CN1 P1a, CN3 P3 -> bam "Truy vet".
  Anh chup luu o data/_demo_snapshot/ (tao lai: tools\chuan_bi_demo.py --luu khi live o diem mong muon).
